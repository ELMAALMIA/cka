    How to study with this guide

    1. Read a lesson once, then take its quiz without scrolling back. Aim for 80% before moving on.
    2. For every wrong answer, read the explanation and run the command on a real cluster such as kind, minikube or a kubeadm VM.
    3. Pair each topic with the matching hands-on lab in this repository's labs folder. The last topic lists which lab goes with which topic.
    4. Once every segment of the wheel is green, take the mock exam until you pass it comfortably.
    The real exams are hands-on. The CKA and CKAD are two hours of tasks in a terminal, with no multiple choice. Use these quizzes to check your understanding, and spend most of your time typing kubectl.
    Scores are stored only in this browser.
